HACKATHON PROTOTYPE

What this product actually shows

It organizes eight real radar observations into one timeline. Fiona and July 2023 use comparable RCM pairs; Dorian and Lee use matched Sentinel-1 pairs. All four periods support calculated low-return change candidates.

  1. ObserveMove through real satellite snapshots from 2019 to 2023.
  2. MeasureInspect orange areas that newly crossed a shared water-like radar threshold within each matched pair.
  3. ValidateCombine candidates with flood records, terrain, permanent water and local evidence.
ImportantThis is a screening tool. It does not prove that a place flooded and it is not an engineering, insurance or property-value assessment.
QUICK GUIDE

How to use the timeline

  1. 1. Start in 2019Move the slider to Dorian · before, or press play to begin the full sequence.
  2. 2. Move through timeThe eight stops show Dorian, Fiona, the July 2023 rainfall interval, and Lee in date order.
  3. 3. Watch the mapThe black-and-white radar image changes at every stop. Each before/after pair uses one shared display scale.
  4. 4. Read the badgeThe badge identifies the event, date, sensor and measured interval.
  5. 5. Study measured intervalsDorian and Lee use matched Sentinel-1 pairs; Fiona and July use matched RCM pairs. Orange means newly water-like; blue means both dates; purple means the first date only.
  6. 6. Adjust visibilityUse “Radar visibility” to reveal the street map beneath the satellite image.
  7. 7. Inspect resultsClick an orange shape or select a large candidate from the left panel.
RememberThese are candidate radar changes, not confirmed flood boundaries. The July 21 image was captured during rainfall onset, not after the complete event.
